What the map can tell us

Broad elevation patterns, sampled slopes, relative route time / energy estimates, and comparisons under explicit vehicle assumptions.

WHAT THE MAP CANNOT VERIFY

Rocks. Sand. Traction. Weather.

Small-scale hazards, ground strength, wheel performance, communications windows and real spacecraft operating constraints require other data and validation.
